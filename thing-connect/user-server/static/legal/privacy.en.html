XiaoTai Console Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

1. Scope2. Information processed and purposes3. Permissions and choices4. Service providers and information sharing5. Storage, retention and security6. Your rights and account closure7. Minors, updates and contact

1. Scope

This policy applies to XiaoTai Console provided by Shenzhen Tange Intelligent Technology Co., Ltd. (“we” or “us”). The platform is intended for developers, partners and technical support personnel for device management and audio/video and AI debugging and demonstrations. Development and demonstration use does not affect your statutory personal information rights.

We process personal information only as needed to provide the features you use, fulfill legal obligations or within the scope of necessary consent. Authorization for optional features is not a prerequisite for unrelated features.

2. Information processed and purposes

  • Accounts: Email addresses, password hashes, verification codes and account status for registration, login, password recovery and access control.
  • Devices and communications: Device IDs, MAC addresses, names, models, firmware and media capabilities, online status, binding relationships, and contact, room and session information for device management and authorized communications.
  • Audio and video: Audio/video and connection information needed for live viewing, intercom or calls, processed only when you use the corresponding feature.
  • AI features: Role configurations, prompts, input audio and text, knowledge-base files, analysis results and invocation records for Qwen analysis, conversations and retrieval. Custom tools also process invocation parameters you configure.
  • WeChat integration: OpenID, WeChat session identifiers, device authorization and VoIP call information when you use the relevant features.
  • Security information: IP addresses, access times, operation results and necessary fault records for authentication, abuse prevention and security audits. NetEase Yidun also processes browser, network and interaction information needed to complete verification.
  • Local storage: Your browser stores login credentials, language preferences and device-list snapshots. Logging out clears login credentials and related snapshots. You may also clear local data in your browser.

3. Permissions and choices

The microphone is used for voice intercom or calls that you initiate. Access to the local camera is requested only for supported scanning or video features. Viewing a remote device does not require permission to use the local camera. Web registration, login and routine device management do not require location or Bluetooth permissions. Permission purposes for features such as mini-program network setup are explained separately.

You may withdraw permissions in browser, system or WeChat settings. Refusing or withdrawing permission affects only features that depend on it and does not affect lawful processing before withdrawal. Browser permissions do not replace necessary notice and authorization for device users and people whose information is collected.

4. Service providers and information sharing

  • Alibaba Cloud infrastructure and email services: Servers, storage and delivery of verification emails, involving relevant business data, recipient email addresses, email content and delivery information. Account passwords are not provided for sending email.
  • Alibaba Qwen: Audio, text, prompts and related context required for AI requests, returning analysis or conversation results. See the Alibaba Cloud Model Studio privacy notice for upstream processing and retention.
  • NetEase Yidun: Human verification, processing verification results and necessary browser, network and interaction information. See the NetEase Yidun Privacy Policy.
  • TiRTC and WeChat: Real-time communications, mini-program identity and VoIP capabilities, processing relevant device, session and authorization identifiers and communication data.
  • Custom tools: Plugins or MCP services you enable receive necessary parameters according to configuration. Check the recipient and permissions before enabling them.

We agree on processing scope and protection obligations with entrusted processors and supervise them as required by law. When providing information to independent personal information processors, we disclose the recipient, contact details, purposes and information categories and fulfill applicable separate-consent requirements. Providers’ own policies do not replace our notice and protection obligations.

When personal information is transferred due to a merger, restructuring or similar event, we notify you of the recipient as required by law and require continued protection obligations. Changes to processing purposes or methods require renewed authorization where required by law.

5. Storage, retention and security

The platform’s own business data is stored in Alibaba Cloud’s Shanghai region within China. Data processed by third-party services is governed by their service rules. If personal information needs to be provided outside China, we will give separate notice and fulfill applicable cross-border and separate-consent requirements as required by law.

  • Accounts, device relationships and configuration: Retained while providing the relevant services. Deletion and account closure requests are handled under section 6.
  • Knowledge bases and files: Retained for ongoing retrieval until deletion or cleanup after service termination. Unlinking does not delete a file.
  • Original recognition recordings: Temporarily stored only for the current task and removed from the task when it ends. Analysis results and task records are retained separately for queries and troubleshooting; you may request deletion.
  • Generated speech: When enabled, used only for playback of the current result and not provided as historical audio records.
  • Verification codes: Governed by the validity period stated in the email and invalid after expiry.
  • Logs and backups: Retained only as long as needed for security, recovery and statutory retention. Network logs subject to mandatory retention are kept for at least six months; this does not extend the business retention period of original recordings.

When processing purposes have been fulfilled or retention is no longer necessary, we delete or anonymize data as required by law. Data that must legally be retained or cannot yet technically be deleted, including unexpired backups, is subject only to necessary storage and security protection and is deleted when possible. Deleting business data does not mean upstream copies or backups are deleted immediately.

We use security measures such as authentication and access control. If a personal information security incident occurs, we take remedial measures and fulfill notification and reporting obligations as required by law.

6. Your rights and account closure

You may manage devices, contacts, roles and files through available pages, or email openapi@tange.ai to request access, copies, correction or deletion of personal information, withdraw consent or close your account.

We recommend requesting closure from your registered email address and specifying “Account closure”. After verifying identity and confirming the scope, we will promptly communicate the handling arrangements and outcome, explaining any request we cannot fulfill. Do not send passwords, verification codes or device keys.

Account closure may make device management and associated resources unavailable. Back up necessary information first. We delete or anonymize relevant personal information within the agreed scope and coordinate with entrusted service providers. Statutory retention and data that cannot yet be deleted are handled under section 5. Logging out, unbinding a device or revoking WeChat authorization does not close the account.

7. Minors, updates and contact

This platform does not provide services directed at children. Processing personal information of minors under fourteen requires guardian consent and special protective measures as required by law. Contact us if you discover processing without appropriate authorization.

Material changes to this policy will be communicated appropriately. Publishing a new version does not replace your consent where changes in processing require renewed consent.

Operator: Shenzhen Tange Intelligent Technology Co., Ltd. For service inquiries, complaints, personal information requests and account closure, contact openapi@tange.ai. Website: www.tange.ai.
